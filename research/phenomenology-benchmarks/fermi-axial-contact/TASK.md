# From the axial-lepton contact Lagrangian to Fermi-LAT dwarf limits

Question: for a stable, symmetric Dirac fermion with a vector dark current
and the axial L_mu-L_tau charged-lepton / left-handed neutrino current,
what magnitude of the single dimension-six coefficient is excluded by the
public six-year 15-dwarf likelihood, with prompt photons only?

Freeze scope: leading tree s-wave, 2–100 GeV, pure axial charged-lepton limit,
massless neutrinos, full dark matter density in symmetric chi/anti-chi, standard
DMFit two-body prompt yields, no inverse-Compton, relic constraint or finite
mediator recast. Use pinned parent methodology and same likelihood inputs;
Fig. 1 pure-channel references never enter this limit computation. Output one
model-limit PDF plus exact per-mass/per-energy bins. The separate original-axis
overlay is the already validated pure-channel Fig. 1 result, not a curve in
this new coefficient plane. Semianalytic vs direct likelihood roots at held
out 3.7, 17, 73 GeV within 1e-5, independent gamma traces within 1e-12,
threshold/zero-signal/Dirac-normalization checks. If any gate fails, no limit
publication. Benchmark is not the original no-DM model or a light-mediator recast.
