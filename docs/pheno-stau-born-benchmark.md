# Direct stau: model and partonic event-generation checkpoint

**Question and gate.** Can the exactly unmixed, degenerate 400 GeV stau / 80 GeV pure-bino benchmark generate correctly normalized, conserving Born-level events without editing the published 120/1 card? Acceptance: agree with an explicit spin trace for both chiral states and all five light quark flavors; pass the photon-only normalization, threshold scaling, angular moment, event four-momentum and resonance mass tests. Do not proceed to ATLAS yields with this output alone.

**Model.** Follow the simplified signal definition in [ATLAS arXiv:1911.06660v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.06660v2), §§1,3. This is a generator for just the neutral Drell–Yan, narrow-width decay signal. No production interference between the distinct left/right scalar species; add their cross sections, not amplitudes. The negative scalars have Q=-1 and T³=-1/2 or 0. The neutral gauge vertices follow from the scalar covariant kinetic terms. The pure-bino decay uses one chirality at a time with magnitude sqrt(2) g' |Y| and Q=T³+Y; g'=e/cW, Y_L=-1/2, Y_R=-1. The only modeled open stau decay is tau plus bino, with unit branching ratio as in the simplified benchmark. It is not a complete electroweak spectrum, SLHA/UFO export, or an independent check of other MSSM decay channels.

**Production normalization.** With P_h=(1∓γ5)/2, use C_h=e²[Q_q Q_stau/ŝ + (T³_q δ_hL−Q_q sW²)(T³_stau−Q_stau sW²)/(sW² cW² (ŝ−m_Z²))]. The Z is far off shell within the benchmark domain; width is set to zero. Contract the quark current with the difference of final scalar four-momenta. With spin and color averaging, the squared amplitude is ŝ² β² (1−cos²θ)(|C_L|²+|C_R|²)/(4 N_c), N_c=3. The two-body phase space gives dσ̂/dcosθ=β M²/(32πŝ). At fixed ŝ and initial flavor the normalized angular density is 3(1−cos²θ)/4 and the azimuth is uniform. The independent test constructs the gamma matrices explicitly. This checkpoint fixes alpha=1/128, sW²=0.23122, mZ=91.1876 GeV and mτ=1.77686 GeV as numerical benchmark inputs, not as the ATLAS generator tune.

**Decays.** Independent isotropic two-body decays of the two scalar parents in their own rest frames. The two-body momenta use the exact Källén function with a massive tau. For a single-chirality vertex, the spin sum is y²(m²_stau−m²_χ−m²_τ); combine it with p*/(8πm²_stau) for the partial width. The event includes the tau momenta, but not spin information or simulated tau decays. Do not use it to model hadronic tau acceptance.

```sh
python -m unittest discover -s python-agent/tests -p 'test_stau_born.py' -v
python python-agent/tools/stau_born.py --energy 1000 --quark u --chirality L --events 128 --seed 431 > stau-uL.json
```

Distinct initial flavors, scalar chiralities, and fixed partonic energies are separate samples, not proton–proton distributions. Output momenta are (E,px,py,pz) in GeV, rates in fb; keep the same CLI inputs to replay. No PDFs, shower/ISR, tau spin correlations, detector response, official signal patches, K-factors, signal/control counts, CLs, or extrapolated exclusion enter this checkpoint. The next gate needs a PDF-convolved and showered hadronic generator with spin-aware tau decays before detector-level validation.

**Replay evidence.** The two raw 16-event files under `docs/validation/stau-born/` use the CLI above with `--events 16` for `u L` and `d R`. The independent trace spans 40 cases (four energies, five flavors, two chiralities); maximum relative difference 3.65e-12, at the near-threshold point. At 1 TeV partonic energy the u-initiated left/right cross sections are 1.44422808/0.71701949 fb. Those are not 13 TeV proton cross sections. The generated decay partial widths are 0.46825436 and 1.87301742 GeV. The raw replay in the same environment is byte-identical.
