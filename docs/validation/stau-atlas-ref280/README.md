# ATLAS SUSY-2018-04: 280/1 GeV stau validation checkpoint

**Pass:** generation of six hard-process classes and native CKKW–L interface smoke. **Not passed / not run:** calibrated matching to the ATLAS sample, detector response on our merged events, independent three control region yields, a five-channel limit.

## Published detector targets

HEPData [v2/t17](https://doi.org/10.17182/hepdata.92006.v2/t17) is the public detector-level cutflow for the 120/1 and 280/1 GeV benchmarks. At 280/1 the final *weighted* signal yields are 6.08 ± 0.79 events (low) and 14.35 ± 1.13 (high), with 278 and 691 raw MC entries, not observed counts. HEPData [t11–16](https://doi.org/10.17182/hepdata.92006.v2/t11) separate acceptance, conditional efficiency and their product. At 280/1, A×ε is 0.52989% and 1.2502%. Final cutflow divided by the weighted all-event denominator closes to the grids to within 2.64e−4 relative. Use matching cut order for stage-by-stage comparisons; do not compare an early trigger from one cut order to the late trigger in another. [t18](https://doi.org/10.17182/hepdata.92006.v2/t18) lists observed/background counts for all five regions, not an independent detector signal cutflow.

The independent authors' [MA5 recast](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.02245), §7.3/Tables 7.2–3, uses MLM, not ATLAS CKKW–L, and explicitly normalizes its yield after the baseline cut to ATLAS. Its (280,1) signal-region results after that normalization are 5.64 and 13.37, respectively −7.24% and −6.83% relative to t17. At (120,1) the final offsets are +49.80% and +14.76%, and the last mT2 conditional efficiencies differ by approximately +43.6% and +30.8%. These author-reported recast outputs are diagnostics, not independently validated efficiencies for our generated samples; do not fit our baseline to the desired yield.

## Native generator result

ATLAS [arXiv:1911.06660v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.06660v2), §3.2 used two stau eigenstates plus up to two partons, NNPDF2.3 LO, Pythia 8.186 A14 and CKKW–L. Our isolated, nonproduction MG5 3.5.17 source commit (see SHA inventory) natively generated 1,000 equal-weight, stable-stau hard events at 13 TeV using LHAPDF 6.5.5 / NNPDF23_lo_as_0130_qed/0. The six counts L0/L1/L2/R0/R1/R2 are 328/251/132/128/104/57. No photon PDF contribution. All events conserve four-momentum at the printed precision, maximum residual scaled by incoming energy 5.48e−11. In this local production-only SLHA check only the relevant neutral DY inputs were frozen; this card is not the deposited ATLAS generation card or a validated full SUSY spectrum. The 20 GeV matrix-element cutoff is a smoke-test setting, not a claim about ATLAS's matching-scale implementation.

Using native Pythia 8.317 A14 and its documented CKKW–L machinery with nJetMax=2, split the six subprocesses (same momenta, IDs, colors, SCALUP; convert the equal-weight per-process interface to IDWTUP=3 and retain each original subprocess cross-section entry). Run the pre-cut-estimation and shower phases at two diagnostic kT scales. At 20/40 GeV, 979/737 events pass the Pythia merging-scale cut; 410/471 carry nonzero weight in the showered checkpoint. All six classes retain nonzero weights at both scales. No claim of matched cross-section stability from those raw counts: the correct per-class post-cut normalization and statistics come next. No parent/τ decays, hadronization, detector response or ATLAS-specific generator filter is included in this merging test; do not combine it with earlier separately qualified Born tau handoff as if it were a validated detector event record. A differential jet-rate closure check and matched tau decays must precede detector-level yields.
